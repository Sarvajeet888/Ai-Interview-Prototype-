01 / Problem & objective

Practice needs more than a question list

A repeatable session connects preparation with feedback.

Unstructured preparation

Question lists do not recreate a complete interview session.

Little immediate feedback

Students may not know which ideas their answers missed.

No record to revisit

Earlier answers and improvement areas are easily lost.

Our objective

Create a simple web app for guided interview practice with clear, reviewable feedback.

Choose a relevant track

Web development, Java & OOP, or HR.

Practice and review

Timed prompts, reference answers and saved reports.

A learning prototype; no measured hiring or confidence outcomes are claimed.

A simple workflow, built with web fundamentals

HTML forms + validation

CSS handles responsive layout and transitions. JavaScript handles events and updates the DOM.
